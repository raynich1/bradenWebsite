Consulting (testing) work

The CME sometimes accepts mechanical testing work as a source of income, and I have historically been tasked with performing the work for the lab. Clients include both other universities and industrial partners. I have done tensile, cantilever bending, and fracture toughness testing as consulting jobs. This includes everything: sample extraction/preparation, data collection, analysis, interpretation, and report writing to present results.
