Teaching assistant work

Rondebosch, Cape Town | Jan 2025 – Dec 2025

In 2025, I was a teaching assistant for the year-long course "MEC4124W: Product Design". It's essentially the senior design course, where the students are allocated into groups and tasked with designing some device. In this case it was a positive displacement pump. In the first semester I was responsible for guiding them through the design process from brainstorming to creating manufacturing drawings. June and July was spent procuring parts and 3D printing parts for their designs, and then the second semester was dedicated to helping them assemble their designs and troubleshoot problems.
