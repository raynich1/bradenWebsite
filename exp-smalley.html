Job shadow at Smalley Springs and Rings

Lake Zurich, IL | Jan 2020

I spent a week at Smalley, a spring and retaining ring manufacturer in Lake Zurich, IL, as a job shadow experience. I was shadowing the engineers designing the springs, where they would determine crucial spring parameters and put examples through fatigue tests. One of the interesting takeaways was how they had integrated all the departments of their company, as the manufacturing facilities were on the same site as the engineers. There was clearly a good working relationship between the departments, probably facilitated also by the fact that it was a relatively small company.
