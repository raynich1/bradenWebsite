A summary

I am originally from Streamwood, IL, born to an American father and a South African mother. My affinity for mechanical things led me to begin studying mechanical engineering at Northern Illinois University where my time was split between studying (3.701 GPA, Honors program), playing rugby, doing campus ministry, and working at Lowe's (since COVID-19 made learning less efficient).

After 2021, I moved to finish my degree at the University of Cape Town in South Africa, a top-ranked institution which I found to be intense and challenging. It was also made possible due to the degree's validity in the United States through the Washington Accord. The experience exposed me to many new things academically and socially.

During my undergrad I was offered a place at the Centre for Materials Engineering at the university, working on projects which proved to be highly educational—see the experience page. I finished undergrad with the South African equivalent of an A average and won the class award for the best final-year project. Towards the end of my undergrad, the director of the Centre offered me a dissertation-based Masters position (still mechanical engineering, but in a materials science laboratory). I started that in January 2025, and it is nearing completion towards the end of 2026. For the future, I am looking for engineering work that will continue to challenge me and engage my mind.
