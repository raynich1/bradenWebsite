Electromagnetic/thermal simulations

I found ANSYS Electronics to be powerful in combining an induction heating simulation (using their eddy current solver in Maxwell 3D) with a heat transfer/convective cooling simulation (in Icepak), just like the setup in the Gleeble. It would find eddy currents, solve for a power loss distribution, and then using the cooling characteristics solve for a steady-state temperature distribution. What this couldn't account for was radiation, so I did not take the temperature values as trustworthy, but it was valuable for my education and gave me an idea of the power loss distribution I could expect in my parts. Crucially, it showed me how well the coils' cast iron outer shields limited the extent of the magnetic field—this meant I could put components very close to the coils without directly heating them.
