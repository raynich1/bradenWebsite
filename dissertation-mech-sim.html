Mechanical simulations

I knew fellow Masters students whose entire projects revolved around contact stress simulations—it was obvious that refining a simulation to give me accurate contact stress results would be an enormous effort for litte reward. Therefore, design was done based on data from literature and conventional principles. Still, I spent some time in ANSYS Mechanical learning about the pros and cons of various meshing strategies/element types and problem definitions. I got to see firsthand how valuable it is to be able to simplify geometry for the sake of a cleaner mesh and a faster solution.
