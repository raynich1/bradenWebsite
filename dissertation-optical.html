Optical measurement methods

Part of my dissertation was about applying novel methods to fracture toughness testing. One such method involved using digital image correlation (DIC) to measure both sample displacement and the fracture toughness parameter (J-integral). In short, DIC uses a high-contrast speckle pattern and cameras to track surface displacements and thus strain and stress. The below is an example of a speckle pattern I applied to a surface.

Displacement maps such as the one below (taken with MatchID) can then be generated, from which lots of useful information can be taken.

In my opinion, DIC-based fracture toughness testing provides some significant advantages over standard methods, both in fracture toughness measurement and in high-temperature applications. This is all discussed in my dissertation. Regardless, the project was a great opportunity to learn about optical measurement techniques.
